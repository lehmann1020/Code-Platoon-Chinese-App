# Component: Toast
Two variants in an `aria-live="polite"` region at top-center.
- **Definition:** hanzi (large) + pinyin + English; auto-dismisses after ~3.5s; max 3 stacked.
- **Congratulations:** heading + "Continue" button (or "Play again" on the final set); persistent until clicked; button receives focus.
Enter: slide down + fade 220ms. Exit: 160ms. Reduced motion: fade only.
